Man in the Middle Attack: Principles & Tricks

A hands-on study of MitM attacks — ARP/DNS spoofing, SSL stripping, and credential capture on unsecured networks using Kali Linux, Ettercap, and Wireshark — paired with practical defenses like VPNs and HSTS preload.
